Pune Travel Guide
Knowledge file for RAAHIGO --- Your AI Assistant for Places
Purpose: Curated destination knowledge for an AI outing planner and
RAG prototype. This guide contains general descriptions and planning
suggestions, not live data. Verify opening hours, entry rules, ticket
prices, closures, traffic, and route times through current sources or
MCP tools before presenting them as current facts.

1. Planning Pune Outings
Pune is a city in Maharashtra, India, with a mix of historic forts and
landmarks, temples, gardens, museums, nature destinations, and nearby
hill or lake areas.

When creating an itinerary: - Group destinations by geographical area to
reduce unnecessary travel. - Prefer a relaxed number of stops over an
overly packed schedule. - Consider the user's starting location,
preferred time of day, interests, food preference, trip duration,
mobility needs, and whether they are travelling with children. - Keep
outdoor destinations earlier or later in the day when appropriate,
especially in hot weather. - Treat nearby destinations as possible
combinations, not guaranteed route-efficient stops; confirm actual
travel time with a routing service. - Do not invent live opening hours,
prices, ratings, restaurant availability, or traffic conditions.
2. Destinations by Interest
History and Heritage
Shaniwar Wada
- Category: Historic landmark / Maratha heritage
- Area: Central Pune
- Description: A well-known historic fortification associated with
  the Peshwas.
- Good for: History, architecture, heritage-focused city outings.
- Planning note: Can be combined with other central Pune heritage
  locations.
Aga Khan Palace
- Category: Heritage / historical museum
- Area: Yerawada
- Description: A significant historical building associated with
  India's freedom movement.
- Good for: History, architecture, educational visits,
  photography.
- Planning note: Check current visitor timings and any
  restrictions before visiting.
Lal Mahal
- Category: Historic landmark
- Area: Central Pune
- Description: A historic site connected with Chhatrapati Shivaji
  Maharaj and Pune's history.
- Good for: Short heritage stops.
- Planning note: Confirm current visitor access.
Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum
- Category: Museum / culture
- Area: Shukrawar Peth
- Description: A museum known for its collection of Indian art,
  objects, and cultural artifacts.
- Good for: Culture, history, indoor activities.
- Planning note: Allow enough time to explore; verify current
  hours.
Spiritual and Religious Places
Dagdusheth Halwai Ganpati Temple
- Category: Temple / spiritual
- Area: Central Pune
- Description: One of Pune's prominent Ganesh temples.
- Good for: Spiritual visits and a central-city outing.
- Planning note: Crowds can vary significantly, especially during
  festivals.
Parvati Hill and Temple
- Category: Temple / viewpoint / heritage
- Area: Southern central Pune
- Description: A hill with temples and views over parts of Pune.
- Good for: Spiritual visits, city views, light outdoor activity.
- Planning note: The climb includes steps; consider fitness and
  accessibility needs.
Chaturshringi Temple
- Category: Temple / spiritual
- Area: Near Senapati Bapat Road
- Description: A hill temple dedicated to Goddess Chaturshringi.
- Good for: Spiritual outings.
- Planning note: Check access, crowd conditions, and steps before
  planning.

Empress Garden
- Category: Garden / nature

Pune-Okayama Friendship Garden (Pu La Deshpande Garden)
- Category: Japanese-style garden
- Area: Sinhagad Road

- Good for: Scenic walks, photography, relaxed visits.

Vetal Tekdi
- Category: Hill / nature / walking
- Area: Western Pune
- Description: A popular hill area used for walking and enjoying
  city views.
- Good for: Morning walks, nature, light trekking.
- Planning note: Use suitable footwear and check local access and
  safety guidance.
Taljai Hill
- Category: Hill / nature / walking
- Area: Southern Pune
- Description: A green hill area popular with walkers.
- Good for: Nature and outdoor activity.
- Planning note: Plan in daylight and follow local safety
  guidance.

3. Nearby Day-Trip Ideas
These places are outside central Pune or require a separate travel-time
check. Do not combine them with a dense Pune city itinerary without
checking the route and available time.
Sinhagad Fort
- Category: Fort / history / hill outing
- Direction: Southwest of Pune
- Description: A historic hill fort known for its views and
  association with Maratha history.
- Good for: History, nature, trekking, scenic outings.
- Planning note: Check road conditions, weather, access, and local
  restrictions.
Khadakwasla Dam
- Category: Lake / scenic drive
- Direction: Southwest of Pune
- Description: A reservoir area often included in scenic drives
  near Pune.
- Good for: A short scenic stop.
- Planning note: Follow safety signs and avoid unsafe water
  access.
Lavasa
- Category: Hill-town / scenic outing
- Direction: West of Pune
- Description: A planned hill-town area near the lakes and hills
  of the region.
- Good for: Scenic drives and a longer outing.
- Planning note: Confirm access, road conditions, and current
  visitor facilities.
Lonavala
- Category: Hill station / nature
- Direction: Southeast of Mumbai and northwest of Pune
- Description: A popular hill-station destination with viewpoints
  and seasonal greenery.
- Good for: Full-day trips, nature, monsoon scenery.
- Planning note: Traffic can be heavy on weekends and holidays;
  check live route conditions.

5. Food Preference Handling
RAAHIGO may receive one of these preferences: - Vegetarian -
Non-vegetarian - Either / both

This guide does not certify individual restaurants as vegetarian or
non-vegetarian. Use a live restaurant or places search to find suitable
options near the itinerary. Confirm menu, current operating hours,
dietary requirements, and availability.

When the user prefers vegetarian food: - Prioritize restaurants or
eateries with suitable vegetarian options. - Do not assume that a
restaurant is fully vegetarian unless verified.

When the user prefers non-vegetarian food: - Search for restaurants with
currently listed non-vegetarian options. - Respect any additional
dietary restrictions.

When the user accepts both: - Recommend a convenient, well-reviewed
option based on current data and route proximity.

7. Agent Rules for Using This Guide
1. Treat destination descriptions as general curated knowledge.
2. Use MCP or another current source for live hours, prices, ratings,
   route duration, traffic, weather, closures, and restaurant details.
3. Ask for clarification only when a required planning detail is
   missing; otherwise use reasonable defaults and clearly state them.
4. Do not promise that every listed place can be visited in the
   selected time.
5. Optimize the itinerary geographically using live route information.
6. Include breaks and realistic visit durations when the user requests
   a detailed schedule.
7. If a destination's current access cannot be verified, label it as
   needing confirmation.
8. Never claim that this guide itself contains real-time information.
