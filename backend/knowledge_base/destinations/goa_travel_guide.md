# Goa Travel Guide

## Overview
Goa is a popular travel destination in India, known for its
beaches, Portuguese heritage, food and nightlife.

## North Goa
North Goa is known for its lively beaches, markets,
restaurants and nightlife.

Popular places:
- Baga Beach
- Calangute Beach
- Anjuna Beach
- Vagator Beach

North Goa is suitable for travellers interested in nightlife,
shopping and lively beach experiences.

## South Goa
South Goa is generally associated with quieter beaches,
relaxation and peaceful surroundings.

Popular places:
- Palolem Beach
- Colva Beach
- Agonda Beach

South Goa is suitable for travellers looking for relaxation
and less crowded beach experiences.

## Food
Goa is known for seafood, local Goan cuisine and Portuguese-
influenced dishes.

## Travel Planning
Consider the following when planning a Goa trip:
- Duration of stay
- Preferred beach experience
- Accommodation location
- Transportation
- Food preferences
- Travel budget
